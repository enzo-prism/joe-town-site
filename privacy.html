Updated September 30, 2026

The game

Joe Town does not include analytics, advertising, account creation, or gameplay tracking. Saves are stored locally on each device, and the game can be played offline. The Mac edition is available now; the iPhone and iPad edition is awaiting App Store approval.

The mobile edition offers optional Game Center features handled by Apple. Game Center is not required to play. Towns do not automatically sync between devices.

Purchases and downloads are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.
