What kind of strategy game is Joe Town?

A native city-builder and simulation. Paint roads, tune production chains, make permanent technology choices, and keep an eye on the neighbors. Available for Mac, with an iPhone and iPad edition coming soon.

Is Joe Town available on iPhone and iPad?

The mobile edition is awaiting App Store review. The touch interface shown on this page previews further improvements still in development. Joe Town is available for Mac today.

Which iPhones and iPads will be supported?

The mobile edition is designed for iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 and later, on devices with an A12 chip or newer. Play in landscape on iPhone; iPad supports portrait and landscape.

Is $9.99 the full Mac game?

Yes. The US Mac App Store price is $9.99 once for the full game, including all ten ages, 24 building types and 18 civilization technology choices, plus a branching guild system, named Joes, and local saves. There are no ads or in-app purchases. Pricing can vary by region.

Is Joe Town an idle game?

Joe Town combines hands-on town planning with an economy that keeps working. Town-defining choices wait for you, and bounded offline progress arrives as a report when you return.

Does it play offline?

Yes. Joe Town saves locally and runs with the internet off. Each device has its own town; saves do not automatically sync between devices.

Which Macs are supported?

Version 1.7.1 supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 14 or later.

Is every town the same?

No. Worlds are seeded, founders arrive in a different order, and towns diverge through their Joes and choices.

Does Joe Town collect data?

The game collects no gameplay data and requires no account. This website uses Google Analytics for aggregate visits.
