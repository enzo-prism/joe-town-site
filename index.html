05 The practical matters

Buy it once. Keep the whole game.

What kind of strategy game is Joe Town?

A native Mac city-builder and simulation. Paint roads, tune production chains, make permanent technology choices, and keep an eye on the neighbors — the ducks have a parliament, and the geese consider volume a negotiating skill.

Is $9.99 the full game?

Yes. One purchase, all ten ages, 24 buildings, 18 technologies, every named Joe, local saves. No ads, no in-app purchases, no gameplay tracking. As the flock puts it: “Hoarding, but civic-minded.”

Is Joe Town an idle game?

No — it respects your absence without demanding it. Town-defining choices wait for you; offline progress is bounded and arrives as a tidy report. No streaks, no energy meters, no guilt.

Does it play offline?

Entirely. Joe Town saves locally and runs with the internet off. On your return the town reports in — usually something like “Productivity occurred without supervision.”

Which Macs are supported?

Version 1.6 supports Apple silicon Macs running macOS 14 or later. Support for Intel Macs in the next update is still being verified. Menubar, VoiceOver, and Reduce Motion are built in.

Is every town the same?

No two are. Worlds are seeded, the twelve founders arrive in a different order every game, and towns diverge by what their Joes asked for.

Does Joe Town collect data?

The Mac game collects nothing — no gameplay data, no accounts. This website uses Google Analytics for aggregate visits; see the privacy notice.
